\section{Preface}
{\large It's the best time to study artificial intelligence. When deep learning started taking off around 2014, I was 8 years old. Now, I remember in early 2023 a friend introduced me to ChatGPT, and since then I haven't really had more enthusiasm for anything else than for AI. Of course, the story of AI and the mysteries of intelligence and consciousness continues to unfold.
\\
}

{\large\noindent AI is definitely overhyped. But behind the hype, there is beautiful science and mathematics and engineering fueling this technology.
\\
}

{\large\noindent In the summer of 2026, I needed to learn computer vision for a project I was working on. It took a while, but eventually I took the decision to learn it in a slow and painful way. When I hit the bedrock, I saw that there wasn't any magic, just normal numbers making multiplications using matrices. It felt like I was at the edge of a discovery. That same year I started writing this thing to reinforce and share what I had learned. The hope is that anyone reading this discovers like I did all the core ideas and machinery that make all AI work.}
